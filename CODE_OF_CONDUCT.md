# Code of conduct

Rem is made by people in their free time, for anyone who wants their PC
in their pocket. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience,
background or identity.

When you take part (issues, pull requests, discussions):

- be kind and assume good intent;
- disagree with ideas, not people;
- no harassment, insults, or sexual or discriminatory language;
- don't share anyone's private information.

Something wrong? Contact the maintainer through GitHub. Comments, issues
or contributions that break these rules may be removed, and repeat or
serious offenders blocked from the project.
